✦ The Cardstock manifesto · est. 2026

The tyranny of the blank canvas.

A free-form design tool hands you a thousand choices and asks you to get all of them right before lunch. Most people should not have to. Cardstock makes the choices once, carefully, so you can spend your three minutes on the words.

The core problem

Good posts die in rushed screenshots.

People share real wins, launches and lessons as phone-notes screenshots or hurried canvases. The thinking is good; the presentation makes it easy to scroll past. A card should carry the message, not compete with it.

So every template is built for a single job, with type sizes, spacing and contrast tuned for a phone held at arm's length in a busy feed.

Our philosophy

Three rules we do not break.

1

Design built into code

Layouts are components, not pixels. Your text flows into a structure that already works at 1080 px.

2

Your drafts are yours

Designs stay in your browser. The server only knows who you are and how to sign your cards.

3

No watermark, ever

The export is the product. It will never be held back for an upgrade.

Honest about the stack

Small, fast and boring on purpose.
