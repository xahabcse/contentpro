FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? Open an issue on GitHub.

How do I export for LinkedIn or Facebook?

A single card exports as a 1080 × 1080 PNG. For a carousel, choose a multi-page PDF to post as a LinkedIn document, or a ZIP of 1080 × 1350 PNG slides to upload in order on Facebook.

Why fixed templates instead of a free-form canvas?

Because the layout is already right, you only write. It is faster and every post looks consistent.

Are my designs saved on your server?

No. They live in this browser. Use the JSON backup to move them.

Can I add my own photo, logo and brand colours?

Yes. Set them once in Profile & Company, or override per design.

Is there a watermark on exports?

Never.

Can I try it without an account?

Yes, the demo opens the full studio with sample designs.
