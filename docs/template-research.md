# Template research and differentiation

The 100 template types are **Cardstock's product taxonomy**, not a list of official LinkedIn formats. LinkedIn's guidance describes useful *content* and document presentation; the individual template IDs and compositions are our design decisions. A type should earn its place through a recognizable visual structure and editable fields, not a new title on an existing card.

## Primary sources

| Source | Guidance relevant to the catalog |
| --- | --- |
| [LinkedIn: Upload and share documents](https://www.linkedin.com/help/billing/answer/a518909) | Recommends career advice, culture stories, insights and trends, event content, and knowledge sharing. Recommends a clear document title, PDF where possible, and consistent page sizes. |
| [LinkedIn: CREATE guide](https://members.linkedin.com/create-start) | Suggests career lessons, expertise, industry news, behind-the-scenes processes, thought leadership, and success stories. These support the learning, career, and work-life topics in the catalog. |
| [LinkedIn: Document Ads getting started guide](https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/lem/business/en/advertise/ads/sponsored-content/linkedin-document-ads-getting-started-guide.pdf) | Supports guides, tip sheets, case studies, data sheets, infographics, and how-to material. Its **ad-specific** creative advice calls for a strong cover, images, bullets, graphics, short pages readable on phones, and adequate contrast; it warns against small text and dense multi-column copy. The under-10-page suggestion is for Document Ads, not a platform limit on organic carousels. |
| [Chart.js: line](https://www.chartjs.org/docs/latest/charts/line.html), [bar](https://www.chartjs.org/docs/latest/charts/bar.html), [pie and doughnut](https://www.chartjs.org/docs/latest/charts/doughnut) | Documents distinct data shapes: trends, category comparisons, and proportions. These justify separate chart layouts, but Chart.js does not define LinkedIn post categories or require use of its library. |

## Differentiation rules

| Nearby types | Distinct visual composition and editable data |
| --- | --- |
| `tips`, `bullet_lesson`, `howto`, `quickstart_guide` | `tips`: independent short suggestions. `bullet_lesson`: one concept with supporting bullet hierarchy. `howto`: ordered task steps. `quickstart_guide`: starting goal, prerequisites, numbered actions, and expected outcome. |
| `framework`, `principles`, `mental_model`, `concept_breakdown` | `framework`: named parts in a structured system. `principles`: numbered rules. `mental_model`: central idea and connected implications. `concept_breakdown`: whole-to-parts diagram with labels. |
| `takeaway`, `lessons_learned`, `case_study`, `postmortem` | `takeaway`: one conclusion. `lessons_learned`: several observations linked to actions. `case_study`: challenge, approach, evidence, result. `postmortem`: incident timeline, cause, fix, prevention. |
| `stat`, `bignum`, `stattrio`, `progress_tracker` | Existing layouts already show one statistic, one dominant number/ring, and three metrics. `progress_tracker` needs multiple named stages with separate states or values, not another single progress bar. |
| `line_chart`, `bar_chart`, `pie_chart`, `growthchart` | Line: values over a sequence with an axis. Bar: labeled category magnitudes. Pie: parts of a whole with values or percentages. `growthchart`: time trend plus a period comparison and change callout; its additional narrative must be visible. |
| `surveyresults`, `budgetbreakdown`, `impactscorecard`, `campaignprogress` | `surveyresults`: question, response counts, and distribution. `budgetbreakdown`: named allocations and total. `impactscorecard`: outcome categories against goals or baselines. `campaignprogress`: achieved versus target with a deadline or milestone. Each can use a different chart or diagram, but its source data must remain editable and numerically coherent. |
| `congrats`, `promotion`, `graduation`, `certification` | Celebration is generic; promotion shows previous and new role, graduation shows qualification and institution, certification shows credential, issuer, and date. |
| `event`, `webinar`, `workshop` | Event gives occasion, place, and time. Webinar foregrounds speaker and online session details. Workshop foregrounds hands-on agenda, materials, and learning outcome. |
| `testimonial`, `customerstory`, `mentorappreciation` | Testimonial centers a quote. Customer story shows challenge, intervention, and result. Mentor appreciation centers a named mentor and specific contribution. |

Across types, keep headings and charts legible at phone size, avoid long multi-column text, and use clear contrast. Preview each layout in both square and portrait exports. For charts, labels, values, units, and any percentage totals must come from editable content; decorative example data must never be presented as the user's actual result.
